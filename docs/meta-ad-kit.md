# Meta launch kit

Prepared for the live 402-LAB landing page: https://handsel-mandate-demo.vercel.app

## Campaign draft

- **Objective:** Leads or website visits / landing page views
- **Destination:** https://handsel-mandate-demo.vercel.app/#seller
- **CTA:** Learn More
- **Audience hypothesis:** Korean AI, automation, API, indie hacker, and developer-tool builders. Start with a small test audience; compare qualified landing-page visits and seller-pilot applications before expanding.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements; use the supplied wide image as the feed creative and review the crop in each placement before publishing.
- **Budget and dates:** Set these in Ads Manager after selecting the intended ad account and billing method.

## Korean ad copy

### Primary text

바이브코딩으로 만든 소중한 제품, 사람이 아니라 그 제품을 가장 잘 찾아낼 수 있는 에이전트에게 팔아보세요. 402-LAB은 x402 결제를 붙여 에이전트가 API를 발견하고 호출하도록 연결하는 오픈소스 파일럿입니다. 작동하는 엔드포인트와 예제 입력·출력, 원하는 호출 가격을 보내고 판매자 파일럿을 신청하세요. 연결은 제품별 검토가 필요하며 구매자 유입이나 매출은 보장하지 않습니다.

Use `demo/assets/hero.png`. Keep headline text in the ad fields rather than baking copy into the image. Review the 1:1 and 4:5 placement crops in Ads Manager.

## Review before publishing

- Confirm the selected Meta ad account, business identity, billing method, audience, budget, and schedule.
- Meta may require additional eligibility or review for ads that mention cryptocurrency or financial services. Follow the requirements shown in Ads Manager; do not imply Meta approval or guaranteed campaign delivery.
- Keep the mainnet payment warning visible in the landing page and in the creative preview.
- Do not add customer lists or personal data to targeting.
